Help & about

Using the map

Search for a place, click the map, or use your browser location to choose the centre point. The dashed circle is the active search radius.

Prices and ranking

Choose fuel, service mode and freshness. Filters can limit maximum price and/or straight-line distance. Sort and Show affect the final visible subset used consistently by the map, ranking and price distribution.

Station details

Select a marker or ranking row to focus the same station. Desktop opens a map popup; narrow screens switch the lower sheet from results to station details. History always belongs to the selected fuel and service mode.

Touch controls

Drag and pinch the map normally. The lower sheet holds either results or station details: use Back to return to results, Hide to maximize the map, and the bottom-right reopen control to restore results. The price-distribution row expands on tap.

Freshness and history

Freshness filters each station by its communicated price age. Local history contains only snapshots actually stored for viewed stations; missing days are never interpolated.
